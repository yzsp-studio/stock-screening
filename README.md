## 🇬🇧 English

This skill turns "find me some stocks" into an executable, traceable research process. It covers A-shares, Hong Kong, US and other markets, and handles candidate pool construction, head-to-head comparison of named stocks, and screening by industry, theme, supply-chain segment or strategy style — with leadership split into industry, business, market-cap and trading leaders. Data comes from two lanes: quotes, valuation, financials, index membership and filings go through structured financial retrieval, while products, customers, orders, capacity, policy and supply-chain relationships go through public-source verification, so every core candidate must land on a filing or periodic report rather than a concept tag. Conclusions ship as transparent groupings with stated reasons; recommendation scores, star ratings, purity scores and hidden-weight rankings are explicitly banned, as is deterministic buy-sell advice.

### ✨ What it does

- Covers A-shares, Hong Kong and US markets with single-stock screening, named-stock comparison and pool building
- Four screening paths: industry, theme or concept, supply-chain segment, and strategy style
- Identifies four layers of leadership: industry, business, market-cap and trading leaders
- Concept tags are only for recall; core candidates must be backed by filings, periodic reports or official Q&A evidence
- Returns transparent groupings of core candidates, key players, watchlist, to-verify and risk-flagged names
- Formal reports set a retrieval budget and stop rules first, then self-check against a report quality gate
